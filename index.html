💌 To someone extraordinary

Dear you,

💞 The day I met you, something changed in me.

We did not talk much, but I felt something good and beautiful about you from the very beginning — the way you carry yourself, the calm in your presence, and the kind of energy you bring into a room. I had heard a lot of things about you before meeting you, and all of them turned out to be true.

There is something about you that feels different, something that makes me feel at ease and deeply connected. I can't promise you the whole world, but I promise I will fight with the world for you, because you are someone worth standing up for and choosing every day.

And if I am honest, there is a place in my heart that already feels like home when I think of us. I can see us building a life together, and I believe with all my heart that one day, we are going to get married. I want to be there for you in every season of life — in your joys, your worries, your dreams, and the quiet moments in between.

I promise I will be there for you, through the good days and the hard ones, with love, patience, and my whole heart. You deserve someone who stays, someone who chooses you every day, and I want to be that person.

With all my heart 💕

Your forever person
